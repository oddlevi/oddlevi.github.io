Your own page.
Yours alone.

Your page answers what you are really wondering: am I running easy enough, can my body take more this week, and how far am I from my race? You get the answer in plain words, not in numbers you have to interpret yourself, and your coach sees the same as you. The plan, the calendar, strength, health and your shoes sit below, and everything updates the moment your watch uploads a run. Add the page to your home screen, and it tells you when the plan is adjusted, the coach has written to you or a new run is in. You stop wondering whether you are doing it right.

How you get in

No app. No password. Just your link.

  • Personal link: as a Treni runner you get your own link sent straight to you by email or in your Telegram group. One tap and you are in, and you stay signed in on your phone.
  • Only your page: the link shows you, and only you. No other runner can see your numbers, and you cannot see theirs.
  • Always fresh. Strava or Intervals.icu tells us the moment you upload, and the run is on your page seconds later.

Already a test runner? Your link is in your welcome email. Reply to it if you cannot find it, and you get a new one.

Want your own page?

No waitlist. You get your own page right away, and your plan as soon as your watch is connected and your history is in.
