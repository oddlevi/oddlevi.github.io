Easy should be easy. That is the whole secret.

Most runners go too hard on their easy days. Treni reads every run from your watch and shows whether the week really was easy enough – before it becomes an injury.

How it often goes
How Treni steers it

Time in heart-rate zones through the week. Green is easy, warm colours are hard. Illustration.

The most common cause of injuryWhat stops runners most often is not too little training, but too much, too fast – too many hard sessions or a long run that grows too quickly. Treni increases gradually and speaks up before the signs become an injury.

How it works

From your first run to a finished week

The coach behind it

Eirik's principles, in your week

Eirik Haugsnes has set the rules Treni is built on. He follows how the advice works, and has the final say on the coaching.

Energy for everyday lifeEasy sessions build fitness without draining you. You have energy left for everyday life – and for the hard sessions.
Long runEirik runs the long run mostly easy – so easy that he can talk in full sentences.
Hard sessionsEirik runs his intervals under control – the last one should feel as good as the first.
