Treni is a coach-led training guide for runners on road and mountain trails. An AI reads every run you log on Strava — zones, intervals, elevation, feel — and a coach stands behind every piece of advice you get. One goal: lasting progress without injury.

The problem

Most runners train blind

  • The plan doesn't know you

    Generic programs don't know what your body has actually done in recent weeks. They don't see that last week turned hard, that the long run grew too fast, or that your "easy" has drifted into the grey zone.

  • The injury comes first

    The most common reason progress stops isn't too little training — it's too much, too soon. Overuse injuries announce themselves in the training data before you feel them in your legs. But someone actually has to read the data.

  • Alone between runs

    A coach who sees you every week is reserved for the few. The rest are left with the watch, the Strava graphs and gut feeling — guessing whether they're on the right track.

Road or mountain

Two worlds — one guide fluent in both

🛣️ For road runners

Pace and kilometres rule here: intervals with second-level precision, threshold sessions measured against your own pace, and week-by-week build-up towards your race — 5K, 10K, half or full.

⛰️ For mountain and trail runners

On trails, pace per km is meaningless — time, heart rate and elevation take over. Uphill as its own session, downhill running as its own skill, and total load that counts what the terrain actually cost you.

Common to both: the same safety rules, the same coach — and many runners do both. The guide sees what surface each run was on, and judges it accordingly.

How it works

Simple on both sides

🏃 For the runner

  1. Connect Strava, answer a few questions

    Goal, age and max heart rate — that's all we need to ask. The guide fetches your history, sets your heart-rate zones on the Norwegian Olympic Federation scale, and learns what a normal week is — for you, not for an average runner.

  2. Get a Monday report and weekly plan

    What last week actually was — easy/hard, zones, load — and what the coming week should hold, session by session, shaped around your races.

  3. Train — and be followed up

    You approve each weekly plan with one tap — or request a change, and it's adjusted. The guide watches through the week, and you can ask about anything, any time, in your own group with the coach on the team.

🤝 For the partner

  1. Running groups: your sessions in the plans

    If the club has intervals Tuesday at 5:15 pm, the guide recommends members do the week's interval session there — with time and place — instead of running alone. The group sessions become the hub, not the competitor.

  2. Organizers: runners ready for the start line

    Participants training toward your race get race-specific preparation — course profile, elevation, tapering the last three weeks. Injury-free, prepared runners come back next year.

  3. We build it together

    The pilot is running now and the forms of partnership are open: club sessions in the plans, offers for participants ahead of races, shared content. Get in touch and we'll find the shape.

It's not how much you run that injures you — it's how much more than your body is used to.

The principle behind every single review. Feeling fit is not injury protection.

Concretely, on two levels: your week is measured against the average of the last four and kept inside the safe band (80–130 %) — a precautionary frame for steady progression (Gabbett 2016; Hulin 2016). And every single run is measured against your longest run in the past 30 days — because a 2025 landmark study (5,205 runners) showed that's where the injury risk actually sits. Both guards — together with the coach's eye.

The science

Research at the base — coaching experience on top

The advice isn't guesswork. Every principle in the guide can be traced to published research or documented coaching practice — and we always say which one it is.

  • 80/20 — counted in time

    The world's best endurance athletes place 80–90% of their endurance training in easy zones. The guide measures your distribution every week — in time, not session count — and speaks up when the grey zone steals from the easy. Your room is 76–85% easy, 15–24% quality.

  • No leaps — documented

    A large study of 5,205 runners found roughly 60% higher injury risk when a single run leaps past the longest your body has done in the last 30 days. The guide catches the leaps before they happen — in the plan, not in hindsight.

  • Terrain measured right

    In the mountains, kilometers lie. Trail sessions are judged on time and elevation — up and down — the way coaches with mountain backgrounds actually do it. A "short" run with 1,600 meters of climbing is treated as the hard session it is.

  • Heart rate + feel, not blind faith in the watch

    The guide knows the blind spots of heart rate — slow response on tired days, drift on long runs — and teaches you to never chase heart rate. Your zones are calibrated against your own data and continuously re-checked.

Sources incl.: Sandbakk et al. 2025 (Sports Medicine – Open) · "How much running is too much?" BJSM 2025 · Seiler & Kjerland 2006 · the coaching team's book on mountain running

What you get

Like having a coach who sees every run

Everyone else requires you to open an app. Treni delivers — unprompted, when it matters. That's the difference between a tool and a service.

  • 📅Your weekly plan — ready before the week starts — concrete sessions with pace, heart rate, intervals and rests, built on the week you actually ran. Finish your week by Sunday evening and next week's plan lands the same evening — otherwise Monday morning.
  • 🔕Follow-up without the noise — the engine reads your runs every day, but you hear from us when it matters: your weekly plan on Sunday evening or Monday morning, a status on Thursday — with praise when you nail it and a friendly nudge when the run got harder than planned. The rest of the week: quiet (and My page whenever you want a look).
  • 🤝A named coach — not an app alone. The advice is reviewed by a human who knows you, and health questions always go to professionals, never to an AI.
  • 💬In your language — all guidance in English or Norwegian, your choice.
  • 📊My page — your own training overview — your load in plain words, the bars behind it, and every run with the advisor's note — plus next week's plan, day by day. Personal link, updated through the day.
  • 📅Calendar, your own sessions and strength — move sessions yourself with drag and drop, add strength, trail runs or cycling as your own sessions, and see it all in one plan. Strength comes with the coach's templates and an exercise bank with animations. Plus the race calendar: 400+ races in Norway, and your plan is built towards the one you pick.
  • 👟Your shoes, tracked — tag your shoes on Strava activities and Treni counts the kilometres per pair, warning you when one nears retirement. Before every race you get shoe advice from your own rotation — matched to distance, surface, course profile and the weather forecast. Need a new pair? One tap registers it — and automatic ordering with partner discounts is on the way.
  • 🔒Your data stays yours — stored locally with the coach team, never sold. Disconnect any time. Read the privacy policy.
