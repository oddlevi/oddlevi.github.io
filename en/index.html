Road or mountain

Two worlds — one guide fluent in both

🛣️ For road runners

Pace and kilometres rule here: intervals with second-level precision, threshold sessions measured against your own pace, and week-by-week build-up towards your race — 5K, 10K, half or full.

⛰️ For mountain and trail runners

On trails, pace per km is meaningless — time, heart rate and elevation take over. Uphill as its own session, downhill running as its own skill, and total load that counts what the terrain actually cost you.

Common to both: the same safety rules, the same coach — and many runners do both. The guide sees what surface each run was on, and judges it accordingly.

How it works

Three steps — and the analyst is on the job

  1. 1Connect Strava

    Two taps and a clear consent. We fetch your runs — distance, time, heart rate, intervals — automatically every morning.

  2. 2The engine reviews

    A rule engine with injury prevention as its first priority: load against what your body is used to, easy that is actually easy, hard sessions within the plan.

  3. 3The coach delivers

    Concrete weekly advice in Telegram — number of intervals, rests, heart rate — always reviewed by a real coach. Ask questions any time.

It's not how much you run that injures you — it's how much more than your body is used to.

The principle behind every single review. Feeling fit is not injury protection.

Concretely: every week is measured against the average of the last four. If your week lands at 80–130 % of what your body is used to, you're in the safe band — more than 30 % over, and injury risk climbs. The band builds on training-load research (Gabbett 2016; Hulin 2016) and is used as a precautionary frame — together with the coach's eye.

What you get

Like having a coach who sees every run

Everyone else requires you to open an app. Treni delivers — every morning, unprompted. That's the difference between a tool and a service.

  • Weekly advice every Monday — concrete sessions with pace, heart rate, intervals and rests, built on the week you actually ran.
  • Follow-up without the noise — the engine reads your runs every day, but you hear from us when it matters: your weekly plan on Monday, a status on Thursday — with praise when you nail it and a friendly nudge when the run got harder than planned. The rest of the week: quiet (and My page whenever you want a look).
  • A named coach — not an app alone. The advice is reviewed by a human who knows you, and health questions always go to professionals, never to an AI.
  • Trail or road — mountain runners are guided on time and heart rate uphill, road runners on pace. In English or Norwegian.
  • My page — your own training overview — your load in plain words, the bars behind it, and every run with the advisor's note. Personal link, updated every morning.
  • Your data stays yours — stored locally with the coach team, never sold. Disconnect any time. Read the privacy policy.
